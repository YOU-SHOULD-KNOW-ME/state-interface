# Illustration assets

The `assets/` directory contains 52 AI-assisted pixel-character exports contributed to this community component. They depict the orange-haired character with a sunflower ornament and book. They were extracted from approved complete-character animation sheets and aligned on one canvas.

Contributor-provided code and illustrations are offered under the repository MIT license to the extent of the contributors' rights. This does not grant rights to third-party trademarks, names, logos, or reference artwork. Do not imply official Anthropic sponsorship or endorsement.

The official Claude mascot/squid artwork is **not included**. If a film needs that separate host character, obtain an appropriate reference/asset separately and record its provenance. The package does not include Muse artwork or imply a relationship with its creators.

The PNGs contain no connection settings, prompts, transcripts, source workstation paths, or embedded image text metadata. Exact source-sheet/workstation history is deliberately excluded from the distribution.
